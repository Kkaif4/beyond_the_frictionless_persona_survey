To implement the empirical architecture for *Beyond the Frictionless Persona*, the survey design requires **four distinct questionnaires** containing a total of **24 to 26 standardized items** (depending on demographic variables).

Below is the complete battery of questionnaires, including their academic titles, measurement scales, and the exact list of questions to be administered to the 18–25 demographic.

### 1. Demographic and Inclusion Screening Questionnaire

**Purpose:** To isolate the target demographic (emerging adults) and establish baseline AI usage before administering the primary psychological scales.
**Number of Items:** 4
**Scale:** Multiple Choice / Categorical

1. What is your current age? *(Exclusion criteria: under 18 or over 25)*
2. How often do you interact with generative AI chatbots or AI companions (e.g., Replika, Character.AI, ChatGPT) for personal conversation or emotional support?
3. Which of the following best describes your primary relationship status? (Single, In a committed relationship, Married, Other)
4. On average, how many hours per week do you spend actively messaging or conversing with an AI companion?

---

### 2. The Artificial Intelligence Chatbot Dependence Scale (AICDS)

**Purpose:** To measure the independent variable of systemic psychological dependence on frictionless AI entities, originally validated by Zhang et al. (2025).
**Number of Items:** 8
**Scale:** 7-point Likert Scale (1 = *Strongly Disagree* to 7 = *Strongly Agree*)

**Questions:**

1. I find myself thinking about my AI companion or chatbot even when I am not interacting with it.
2. I feel a strong, irresistible urge to interact with my AI chatbot when I am stressed, anxious, or upset.
3. I have attempted to reduce the amount of time I spend talking to my AI companion but have been unsuccessful.
4. I feel restless, irritable, or anxious when I am temporarily unable to access my AI companion.
5. I frequently spend more time interacting with my AI companion than I initially intended to.
6. I increasingly prefer talking to my AI companion over spending time with my real-life friends, family, or partner.
7. My reliance on my AI companion has caused friction or problems in my offline human relationships.
8. I use my AI companion specifically as a way to escape from real-world problems or numb negative emotions.

---

### 3. Perceived Emotional Validation from AI (Adapted MSPSS)

**Purpose:** To measure the mediating variable of emotional validation. Because a universal AI-specific scale is still emerging, this utilizes the widely established Multidimensional Scale of Perceived Social Support (MSPSS), with the "Significant Other" subscale specifically adapted to target the AI entity.
**Number of Items:** 4
**Scale:** 7-point Likert Scale (1 = *Very Strongly Disagree* to 7 = *Very Strongly Agree*)

**Questions:**
*Prompt provided to user: "Think specifically about the AI chatbot or companion you interact with most frequently when answering the following."*

1. My AI companion is always around when I am in need of emotional support.
2. I have a safe space to share my deepest joys and sorrows with my AI companion.
3. My AI companion is a real and highly effective source of comfort to me.
4. I feel that my AI companion genuinely understands and cares about my feelings.

---

### 4. The Dutch Test for Conflict Handling (DUTCH) - Interpersonal Relationships

**Purpose:** To measure the two primary dependent variables: Conflict Avoidance and Collaborative Conflict Resolution in offline, human-to-human dynamics.
**Number of Items:** 8 (4 items for the *Avoiding* subscale; 4 items for the *Problem Solving* subscale).
**Scale:** 5-point Likert Scale (1 = *Not at all* to 5 = *Very much*)

**Questions:**
*Prompt provided to user: "Think about how you typically handle disagreements, arguments, or friction with your real-life human friends, family, or romantic partners."*

**Subscale A: Avoiding (Conflict Avoidance)**

1. I actively avoid confrontations about our differences.
2. I avoid differences of opinion as much as possible.
3. I try to make our differences loom less severe rather than addressing them directly.
4. I try to avoid any confrontation with the other person.

**Subscale B: Problem Solving (Collaborative Resolution)**
5. I examine issues deeply until I find a solution that really satisfies me and the other party.
6. I stand up for my own goals and interests while also actively supporting the other person's goals.
7. I examine ideas from both sides to find a mutually optimal solution.
8. I work out a solution that serves my own as well as the other's interests as well as possible.
